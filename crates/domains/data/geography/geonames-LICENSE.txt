## Data license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)

GeoNames.org's export terms state:

  "The GeoNames Gazetteer Data is licensed under a Creative Commons
   Attribution 4.0 License. You should give credit to GeoNames when using
   data or web services with a link or another reference to GeoNames."

  <https://www.geonames.org/export/>

This praxis source carries only DATA fetched from GeoNames
(`download.geonames.org/export/dump/countryInfo.txt`, verbatim raw bytes,
no code), so only the data license applies.

Suggested attribution: "Contains information from GeoNames
(https://www.geonames.org/), which is made available here under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)."

## In this codebase

- Registered source: `[sources.geonames_countryinfo]` in `praxis.toml`.
