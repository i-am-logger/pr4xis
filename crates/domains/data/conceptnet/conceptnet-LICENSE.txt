## Provenance of the filtered subset shipped in this repository

`crates/domains/data/conceptnet/conceptnet-5.7.0.assoc` is NOT the full
ConceptNet assertions graph. It is a mechanical subset of the official
conceptnet-assertions-5.7.0.csv.gz release
(https://s3.amazonaws.com/conceptnet/downloads/2019/edges/conceptnet-assertions-5.7.0.csv.gz),
filtered to the 943,308 (of 34,074,917) assertions whose start AND end
concepts both resolve to a lemma present in the loaded Open English WordNet
2025 — a pure format-conversion + membership filter of the original relation/
start/end/weight fields, never a reweighting, re-ranking, or editorial
interpretation of the source data. See `regenerate.rs`'s module doc for the
exact filter and its literature/URL citations.

Per the ShareAlike term, this derived subset is distributed under the same
CC-By-SA 4.0 license as the original.
